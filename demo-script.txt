LEARNBRIDGE AI DEMO SCRIPT

INTRODUCTION

Every year, millions of students across Nigeria prepare for WAEC and JAMB examinations.

However, many students struggle because they lack access to quality tutoring and personalized learning support.

LearnBridge AI was built to help bridge this educational gap using local AI powered by Gemma 4.



PRODUCT OVERVIEW

LearnBridge AI is an AI-powered tutoring assistant that helps students:
- understand difficult concepts
- generate practice quizzes
- prepare for WAEC and JAMB examinations

The application runs locally using Ollama and Gemma models.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

The application was built using:
- Streamlit
- Ollama
- Gemma 4
- Python

The project uses local AI inference to support accessibility in low-resource environments.



FUTURE VISION

Our vision is to expand LearnBridge AI into an offline-first educational platform with multilingual support, voice tutoring, and personalized learning paths.

LearnBridge AI is more than an app.

It is a step toward making quality education accessible to every student.
